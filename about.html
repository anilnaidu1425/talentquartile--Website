Who We Are

TalentQuartile LLP is a recruitment and technology services company established in October 2026, with its headquarters in Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

We focus on connecting organizations with the right talent through effective recruitment, Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO), staffing and IT services.

Our Vision

To become a trusted partner for organizations seeking quality talent acquisition, workforce solutions and technology services.
